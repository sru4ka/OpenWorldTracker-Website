Delete your data

This page explains how to delete the data Open World Tracker holds about you.

Data on your device

Open the app, go to the Profile tab and tap "Reset all progress". Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your device.

Data on our servers

If you have used community features (pins, votes, tips or public chat), email dbromberg1@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my data" and your display name in the app. We will delete your account identifier, display name, points, pins, votes, tips and chat messages within 30 days.

Anonymous vote totals that your votes contributed to may be recalculated without your votes.

What we do not hold

We do not hold your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or payment details.
