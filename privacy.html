Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Open World Tracker ("the app") is an unofficial fan-made companion guide. This page explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your checklist progress, display name and settings are stored on your device.

Information you share with other players

When community features are switched on, the following is stored on our servers (hosted by Supabase) and is visible to other players: your display name, the pins you drop, your votes' effect on totals, the tips you post on items, your messages in the public chat room, and your points and rank. Please do not post personal information. There are no private messages.

A random account identifier is created for you so your contributions stay linked to you. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm that a purchase is valid. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

If the app shows ads, they are provided by Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier to show and measure ads. On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before any tracking. You can change this in your device settings at any time.

Reports and moderation

You can report or block any player or message in the app. Reported content is reviewed and may be removed, and accounts that break the rules may be banned.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 17 and over.

Deleting your data

You can clear the data on your device with "Reset all progress" in the Profile tab or by deleting the app. To have your contributions removed from our servers, email dbromberg1@gmail.com.

Step-by-step instructions are on the delete your data page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Open World Tracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
