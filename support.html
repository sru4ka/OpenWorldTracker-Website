Common questions

A location is wrong. Open the item and vote "No" under "Is this info right?". Three "No" votes hide it.

How do I restore Pro? Open Profile, tap "See Pro", then "Restore purchase".

How do I report someone? Tap the three dots next to their message and choose Report or Block.

How do I delete my data? Use "Reset all progress" in the Profile tab, or email us to remove your contributions from our servers.
